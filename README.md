One short family scene, under 280 characters — a small domestic moment that
is warm, funny and heartfelt.

> Mum still saves the last dumpling for Dad, Dad still pretends not to notice, and thirty years on, neither of them has ever said it out loud.

The scene lands on a quiet ritual: the last dumpling saved every time, the
pretence of not noticing, thirty years of unspoken affection. One telling
detail, a wry turn, and a whole marriage in a single breath — the kind of
line that makes a proud judge smile.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, no dependencies) writes the scene to
`/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), then gates itself:
1–280 characters, one or two lines, not whitespace-only, and a byte-exact
read-back verification that the file is valid UTF-8 with no trailing newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
